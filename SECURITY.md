# Security policy

## Report a vulnerability

Please **do not open a public issue** for a security problem.

Email **security@amazen33.dev** with:
- the affected page or component;
- steps to reproduce;
- the impact you expect;
- whether you want to be credited.

For vulnerabilities in the Twinfra platform itself, use the same address. The platform repository is `amazen33/twinfra`.

## What to expect

| Step | Target |
| --- | --- |
| Acknowledgement | Within 5 business days |
| First assessment (triage) | Within 10 business days |
| Updates | At least every 14 days until resolved |
| Fix and advisory | Coordinated with you. The default disclosure window is 90 days, or earlier once a fix is released |

## Scope

**In scope:**
- the static site at `https://twinfra.amazen33.dev` and `https://twinfra.pages.dev`;
- this repository's build and deployment.

**Out of scope:**
- denial-of-service testing;
- social engineering;
- physical attacks;
- automated scanner output without a demonstrated impact;
- Cloudflare's own infrastructure. Report that to Cloudflare.

Twinfra's development environment is a private lab and is **not** in scope for testing.

## Safe harbour

If you act in good faith and follow this policy, we will not pursue legal action against you for your research, as far as that is within our control. Good faith means you:
- do not access, change or keep other people's data;
- do not degrade any service;
- stop and report as soon as you reach data that isn't yours;
- give us reasonable time to fix the problem before you disclose it.

This policy follows the product security standard SEC-01.
